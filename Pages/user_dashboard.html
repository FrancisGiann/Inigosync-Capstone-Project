      Booking and payment terms

      Review these booking and payment terms from the full Terms & Conditions before continuing.

      Booking details
      • You must be signed in to a registered customer account. Select a sport or court, date, and time slot; each time slot can be reserved by one customer at a time.
      • Review the booking summary, including the court, schedule, and total cost, before confirming. Provide an accurate name and mobile number so the sports center and its staff can confirm, follow up on, or resolve issues with your reservation.
      • The sports center sets court rates and available sports, which may change. The rate shown when you make the booking applies to that booking.
      Payment and confirmation
      • When online checkout is available, booking payments are processed through PayMongo. Available payment methods depend on what the sports center has enabled. You may pay the deposit percentage set by the sports center or pay the full amount.
      • Any remaining balance is due at check-in using a payment method available and confirmed by the sports center.
      • PayMongo may add a processing fee that varies by payment method. Before authorizing payment, review the fee and final total PayMongo displays for your selected method. See PayMongo’s Terms of Use.
      • An online booking is confirmed only after IñigoSync validates PayMongo’s payment-success confirmation. If online checkout is unavailable, follow the payment instructions shown in IñigoSync or contact the sports center before relying on a booking.
      Attendance, cancellation, and refunds
      • Once made, a booking cannot be cancelled. There is no self-service cancellation option in the dashboard.
      • Completed booking payments are non-refundable and cannot be converted to cashback, store credit, or another form of reimbursement.
      • A fixed 30-minute grace period applies: arrive at Iñigos Sports Center within 30 minutes of your reserved start time. If you arrive more than 30 minutes after it, the booking is automatically cancelled as a no-show and the court is released to other customers. A no-show is not refunded and cannot be rescheduled.

      Online payment transaction fee

      Your payment method may add a transaction fee. Review any fee and the final total on the secure payment page before you authorize payment; your booking is confirmed after payment is verified.
