House Rules

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Help keep Iñigos Sports Center welcoming and ready for everyone's next game.

Check in and respect your schedule

Check your reservation details and check in with the front desk before entering your assigned court, lane, or table. Use the assigned area during your reserved time and let the next group start promptly. Ask staff before extending a session or changing an assigned area.

Play safely

Wear footwear and use equipment appropriate to your sport. Follow posted facility instructions and staff guidance. Report unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, or injuries to staff promptly.

Respect other players

Treat other guests and staff respectfully. Keep walkways and entrances clear, supervise children in your care, and keep belongings away from active playing areas.

Care for the facilities

Use equipment for its intended purpose, return borrowed items to staff, dispose of litter properly, and follow posted directions about food and drinks in playing areas.

Bookings, payments, and attendance

Review the Terms & Conditions for the existing booking, payment, cancellation, and attendance policies. Confirm any unclear charges or arrangements with the front desk before booking. These house rules do not introduce additional fees or change the booking terms.

Need help?

Speak to the front desk or contact +63 928 154 6876, itsrosem8@gmail.com, or Iñigos Sports Center on Facebook.
