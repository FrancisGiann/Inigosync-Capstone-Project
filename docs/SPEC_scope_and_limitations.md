Introduction
Project Context
The use of webbased booking systems has become essential in servicebased industries, particularly in sports and recreational facilities, as these systems provide various benefits such as improved scheduling accuracy, realtime monitoring of facility availability, and enhanced customer convenience.  Through online platforms, users can reserve services at a preferred time and schedule, reducing the need for physical presence. Additionally, booking systems facilitate the streamlining of operations, eliminate human error, and maintain organized records, ultimately improving service quality and customer satisfaction (Febriansyah, 2023; Daza et al., 2026; Cajetas et al., 2026). Digital transformation has been recognized as a key strategy in enhancing operational processes, improving efficiency, and increasing the competitiveness of service-based industries. The Philippine Development Plan 2023–2028 emphasizes the need for organizations to adopt digital systems to streamline processes and improve accessibility of services (National Economic and Development Authority, 2023). In addition, the Department of Trade and Industry (2023) highlights the importance of adopting digital technologies among small and medium enterprises to improve business operations and customer experience, including the use of online platforms for scheduling, reservations, and service management.
Sports centers and recreational facilities will continue to face challenges in managing reservations due to the reliance on traditional booking methods such as walkin reservations, phone inquiries, and manual logbooks. These outdated practices will lead to recurring issues, including double bookings, fake bookings, overlapping reservations and  inefficient recordkeeping. Consequently, sports centers like Iñigos Sports Center will experience operational difficulties such as crowded areas, long waiting times for customers, and disorganized reservation records, which will hinder efficient service delivery and affect overall customer satisfaction. 
Therefore, the proponents will address these concerns by developing a webbased booking management system that will enhance accessibility, improve administrative control, and ensure organized and efficient operations. 
Ms. Rosalinda Driz, serves as the secretary of Iñigos Sports Center in Brgy. Bocohan, Diversion Road, Lucena City, and has been assisting with the business since 2011. Since 2011, the sports center has accommodated customers on a walk-in basis, allowing them to use facilities without prior appointments. At present, Ms. Driz manages reservations and inquiries primarily through the official social media account of Iñigos Sports Center, where customers send messages to check available slots and request bookings. Despite this approach, the system remains largely manual and increasingly difficult to coordinate as the number of patrons grows.
As a solution, the proponents propose IñigoSync aims to provide a centralized, reliable, and technologydriven platform that improves service quality and customer experience. The name merges the sports center’s identity with the concept of synchronization, reflecting the system’s purpose of coordinating schedules efficiently.
Purpose and Description
The proponents will develop a web-based booking management system for Iñigo’s Sports Center, designed to provide a more efficient and convenient way of handling court reservations. IñigoSync is intended to assist the management of Iñigos Sports Center in addressing the challenges encountered in managing customer bookings and court schedules. 
Ms. Driz identified that reservations are handled manually through facebook messenger, phone calls, and walk-in inquiries.The process involves staff members manually handling reservations through social media messages and phone calls. Bookings are managed by different staff without a centralized system, inconsistencies in recording, overlapping schedules, and confusion in monitoring available time slots often occur. This lack of coordination makes it difficult for the management to maintain accurate records and efficiently oversee facility usage. Customers are required to visit or contact the sports center to check availability, making the process time-consuming and inconvenient. Furthermore, manual handling of reservations increases the risk of errors such as double booking and miscommunication between staff and clients, particularly when reservation details are conveyed through social media messages or phone calls. 
To address these issues, IñigoSync will provide a centralized web-based platform where customers can check real-time court availability and reserve preferred schedules using smart devices. The system can be accessed at any time and anywhere, as long as there is an internet connection. This approach enhances convenience while promoting a more organized and systematic reservation process.
The system will also enable the administrator to efficiently monitor bookings, manage schedules, and maintain accurate reservation records through a centralized dashboard that displays realtime updates and organized booking data. By minimizing reliance on manual processes, the system helps prevent double bookings, reduce errors, and improve overall operational efficiency. Additionally, it provides the owner with better control and visibility over daily business operations.
Through the development of IñigoSync, the proposed system aims to modernize the booking process at Iñigo’s Sports Center, enhance customer experience, and streamline operational workflows. The system supports improved scheduling management, reduces confusion, and ensures a more reliable and user-friendly reservation system for both customers and staff.
In the context of sports centers, efficient reservation management plays a crucial role in maintaining smooth operations and ensuring customer satisfaction. Traditional booking methods, such as manual scheduling through phone calls, messaging platforms, and walk-in inquiries, are commonly used but often lead to operational inefficiencies. 
The involvement of multiple personnel in managing manual bookings further increases the likelihood of inconsistencies and miscommunication. Without a centralized system, it becomes challenging to monitor court availability in real time, resulting in inconvenience for customers and additional workload for staff.
The implementation of a web-based booking management system addresses these challenges by providing a centralized and accessible platform for both customers and administrators. Such systems allow users to view real-time availability and reserve schedules without the need for physical inquiries. As a result, customer convenience is significantly improved, and service delivery becomes more efficient.
From an administrative perspective, web-based booking systems enhance control over scheduling, record-keeping, and monitoring of reservations. Moreover, the development of IñigoSync is relevant to the needs of Iñigo’s Sports Center. The system is designed to address existing inefficiencies in the current booking process and to support a more organized, reliable, and technology-driven approach to reservation management.
Objectives
General objectives:
The main objective of the propose system is to develop a web-based application that will manage the scheduling and booking process of Iñigos Sport Center. It will help administrators and customers schedule games, access information on facilities and rates, and stay updated on sports activities offered by Iñigos Sports Center.
Specific Objectives:
Online Booking System
The proponents will design and develop a Web-based booking management system that enables the customers to book. This feature includes selecting a court type, choosing preferred date and time, and monitoring schedules. 
Admin Management System
The proponents will design and develop a system that will enable the administrator to efficiently manage and monitor staff, and court management. The system will also allow Ms. Driz to configure staff accounts such as changing passwords and adding new staff accounts.
Payment Automation 
The proponents will design and develop a payment automation feature that allows customers to complete bookings through e-wallet payment. This feature helps provide faster transactions, avoids double booking, and makes the booking process more convenient for both customers and administrators. 
 Security System 
The proponents will design and develop a security system that aims to protect customer and administrator accounts, personal information, and transactions from unauthorized access. The purpose of this objective is to provide a safe, secure, and reliable booking environment for all users of the system 
Scope and Limitations
Scope 
IñigoSync: A Web-based Booking Management System for Iñigos Sports Center provides a streamlined digital solution to enhance business operations, improve customer service, and ensure efficient management of reservations and facility usage. The system includes the following features:
Landing Page Module - This module serves as the main interface and entry point of the IñigoSync system, designed to introduce Iñigos Sports Center to both new and existing customers. The landing page helps attract potential clients and promotes digital engagement by allowing users to navigate to other parts of the system such as booking, registration, or login.
Home Interface - It will function as the welcoming page that will guide users toward exploring the services offered by the sports center. It will serve as the main interface of the system where users will first interact with the website. It will display the center’s name, logo, and a brief introduction that will represent the identity and purpose of the project. 
Facilities & Pricing – It will display the pricing information, including the hourly rate, rating, and type of court, to provide users with a clear understanding of the cost of each facility. This section will emphasize transparency and convenience by allowing customers to compare rates and select the court that best fits the preferences and budget. 
Business Overview - It will provide a clear background of Iñigos Sports Center as a recreational facility that offers various sports courts and services to the community. It will highlight the center’s objectives, such as promoting active lifestyles, supporting organized sports activities, and ensuring accessible venues for customers.
Login/Sign in Interface – It will function as the gateway that will connect users to the system’s core services. It will allow registered users to authenticate the identity and gain access to personalized features such as booking courts, viewing reservations, and managing transactions.
Users Module - This module enables users to register, log in, and manage the accounts securely. It keeps track of user information and ensures that only authorized individuals can access specific parts of the system through role-based access control, making the system reliable and fully functional for its users.
Login/registration - The Login as User feature will allow existing users to securely access their accounts and manage bookings, view schedules, and monitor transactions. The registration feature will enable new customer user to create an account by connecting their Google account to the system for faster and more convenient access.
Login as admin - The Login as Admin feature will allow authorized personnel to access the system using assigned credentials. Once logged in, administrators will be able to oversee bookings, manage user accounts, update court availability, and monitor overall system operations. 
Terms and Condition agreements - It will define the policies and conditions that users will need to review and accept before creating an account or accessing the system. It will include the Booking Guidelines, Payment Policy, Cancellation Rules, Personal Information Use, and Agreement Requirement, which will govern the proper procedures for booking, payment, cancellation, and data privacy. Through these policies, the system will ensure transparency, accountability, and secure interaction between Iñigos Sports Center and its customers.
Security Module - The Security Module will be responsible for managing all authentication and protection mechanisms within the IñigoSync platform. Its main purpose will be to ensure the safety and privacy of user data by preventing unauthorized access, maintaining secure sessions, and enforcing login validation.
Email Authentication – The system will implement an email-based authentication feature to verify the identity of users through registered email addresses. Upon login, the system will send a one-time password (OTP) to the user’s email, which must be entered to complete the sign-in process. This process enhances account security and prevents unauthorized access, ensuring the integrity of user accounts within the Iñigos Sports Center booking system.    
Single Session - The system will implement a policy that will allow all users accounts to remain active on only one device at a time. When the same account is used to log in to another device, the previous session will automatically be terminated. This feature will prevent multiple simultaneous logins, account sharing, and ensure exclusive and secure access for all users.  
Session Expiration - The system will include a session expiration feature that will automatically log out customer users after a specific period of inactivity. This functionality will help prevent unauthorized access when a user forgets to log out or leaves a device unattended. Once the session expires, the user will need to log in again to continue using the system.
Customer Module – It will serve as the primary interface for users to interact with the booking system. It will allow customers to view available courts, schedules, and rates, as well as manage the reservations and account details. This module will consist of several submodules designed to enhance user convenience, accessibility, and overall system functionality. Each submodule will contribute to providing a seamless booking experience, ensuring that customers can efficiently navigate, reserve, and manage the activities within the platform. 
Dashboard Overview – The system will provide customers with a comprehensive view of available courts, a calendar, and scheduled activities. It will serve as the main interface where users will monitor court availability, view upcoming reservations, and access quick navigation to other features. This submodule will enhance user convenience by presenting essential booking information in one organized display. 
Court Information – The system will display detailed information about each court, including its type, hourly rate, and visual preview. It will also highlight court features and availability status to help customers make informed decisions. This submodule will promote transparency by allowing users to compare facilities and select the most suitable court for preferred sport or activity. 
Booking Management – The system will enable customers to book courts by selecting the desired sport, date, and time. It will generate a booking summary showing the chosen court, schedule, and total cost before proceeding to payment. This submodule will streamline the reservation process and ensure accurate transaction records. 
Receipt/Invoice- Once the payment is done, the system will automatically generate a receipt for the completed transaction that includes booking details such as customer name, court type, downpayment or full payment status and total cost.
Account Settings – The system will allow customers to modify account credentials, such as changing passwords and updating registered mobile numbers. It will include validation features to ensure secure updates and protect user data. This submodule will strengthen account security and maintain accurate customer information. 
User Profile – The system will present customers with booking history, personal details, and logout option. It will display essential information such as name, mobile number, and email address, along with the status of previous bookings. This submodule will provide users with a clear record of activities and facilitate easy account management.
Automatic Cancellation- The system will provide a cancellation feature with a fixed 30-minute grace period for all bookings. Customers are required to arrive at the venue within 30 minutes from the start of their reserved schedule. If the customer fails to arrive within the allowed grace period, the system will automatically cancel the booking and reopen the cancelled time slot for other customers to book. In accordance with the Terms and Conditions Agreement, payments for automatically cancelled bookings will be considered non-refundable.
Staff Module - It will serve as the control interface for staff to oversee customer reservations, walk-in appointments, and transaction records. It will provide tools for monitoring schedules, confirming bookings, and maintaining transaction history. This module will consist of several submodules designed to ensure efficient facility management, accurate record-keeping, and secure staff operations.
Booking Overview – The system will allow staff to view pending reservations and scheduled games for the day. It will include search and filter functions to accommodate online bookings, confirm reservations, and record time-in activities. This submodule will ensure that customer requests are properly validated and organized. 
Walk-In Management – The system will enable staff to accommodate walk-in customers by recording personal details, preferred sport, and schedule. It will also process payments through cash or online payment method, once the payment is done, the system will automatically generate a receipt for the completed transaction.
Court Schedule – The system will provide a calendar view of the sports center, displaying the schedule of courts and availability. This submodule will allow staff to monitor booking across different sports facilities. 
Transaction Records – The system will maintain a transaction history with search and filter functions. It will generate an audit trail that records timestamps, customer actions, and booking status, ensuring transparency and accountability in administrative operations. 
Staff profile – The system will present basic information about the staff member, including name, email address, and contact number. It will also provide a logout function to secure staff accounts and prevent unauthorized access.
Admin Module - The Admin Module will serve as the central interface for the sports center owner to oversee bookings, manage staff accounts, update settings, and maintain court facilities. It will provide a clear overview of operations and ensure that administrative tasks are handled efficiently.
Booking Overview – The system will present a summary of reservations through bar graphs, showing weekly and monthly booking trends. It will also display active administrator accounts, allowing the owner to monitor staff activity and overall system performance. 
Staff Management – The system will enable the admin to manage staff accounts by adding new staff, updating credentials, resetting passwords, and deleting accounts when necessary. It will also include payment configuration features to allow proper setup and management of payment settings. This submodule will ensure proper role assignment, secure access control, and organized payment management within the system.  
Account Settings – The system will allow the owner to update personal information such as name, username, and password. It will provide secure account maintenance features to protect sensitive data and maintain accurate records.  
Court Listings – The system will maintain a list of available courts and allow the owner to add new courts when introducing additional sports facilities. The admin will also be able to update court images and other court details when necessary to keep the information accurate and updated. This submodule will ensure that the booking system remains adaptable to the evolving needs of the sports center.
Limitations 
Network Dependency – The system requires an internet connection to function properly. Both customers and admins must be online to book sessions or update booking statuses. If the internet is slow or unavailable, the system will not operate, which could cause delays or missed reservations.
Single-Branch Limitation – The current version of the system was developed exclusively for the operational needs of Iñigos Sports Center, meaning it is limited to a single location. All administrative functions, activity configurations, bookings, reports, and data storage are centralized and tied specifically to this branch.
