## What to take from the sample

The reference is a long landing-page composition shown in two columns. Its strongest ideas are an opaque compact header; a large real-life photo with a dark text-side gradient; a facts strip at the hero boundary; calm light sections with images and concise cards; an occasional full-width dark photographic band; a simple review area; and one purposeful closing action above a compact dark footer. The strength comes from alternating broad, quiet surfaces and a few image-rich moments, not from adding more cards everywhere.

Use those **backgrounds, spacing, image-to-copy balance, and section transitions**. Do not copy its moving-company imagery, yellow/navy brand colors, service names, process steps, blog posts, claims, statistics, or wording. IñigoSync has live events, court listings, a venue story, verified reviews, booking actions, and real contact details; each keeps its own purpose and data source.

### Visual rules for both modes

- Treat the sample as the spacing and hierarchy reference; dark mode uses the **same structure** and natural-color photos, with surface and overlay tokens changed. Light mode uses only white/off-white/light-gray section surfaces with dark type and orange highlights, alternating light tones to divide sections; no solid black landing section backgrounds in light mode. Dark mode is black/charcoal with white type and orange highlights. Avoid carrying the prior blue/khaki palette into either mode.
- Keep a consistent content width near 1240–1440px and substantial but controlled vertical spacing. Use full-bleed backgrounds on hero/About while aligning their inner content with Featured, Courts, Reviews, and the transparent closing section.
- Give each section one main visual job: hero = venue and booking; Featured = event photo; Courts = scanning options; About = story; Reviews = proof; closing band = visit/contact. Avoid repeated photos, duplicate CTAs, and stacked decorative effects.
- Use orange for primary actions, active states, small labels, and selective emphasis. Keep body text black or white according to mode. Confirm final orange and every text/control contrast pairing after the logo colors are extracted.
- Make images responsive with intentional crops or contained artwork. The logo is never cropped; event photos may vary in aspect ratio, so the featured media must protect important image content and keep the caption legible.

## Design decisions

1. **Logo and brand.** Extract the supplied orange, white, and black IñigoSync artwork from its outer black JPEG background into a transparent master image. Preserve black strokes and black areas *inside* the badge and wordmark; a global black-color removal would damage the design. Clean the perimeter at high resolution and inspect it on white, black, and orange. Export a full transparent lockup and square web icons. The supplied image is the **IñigoSync system/website logo only**. Use it for system navigation, sidebars, loading overlay, footer system brand, favicon/apple icon, manifest, and system social/structured-data references. Preserve the separate `assets/Logo/Logo.png` **Iñigos Sports Center business logo** in the About section and any venue/business-branded context, including policy headers labeled as the sports center. Do not overwrite the business logo or replace it indiscriminately. In the header, pair the complete logo with a separate readable system name as specifically requested, even though the artwork includes a small wordmark. Keep image alternative text meaningful by context. Asset URLs used by external email templates or hosted storage need separate verification before they can be claimed updated.
2. **Header and system lockups.** Keep the present navigation destinations and mobile menu, but make the visible navigation surface fully **opaque** in both modes (`alpha: 1`; no translucent background or backdrop blur). Use black in dark mode and white in light mode, with an orange active/focus accent and enough separation from the hero image. Show the **full, uncropped** supplied system logo at a visibly larger size, with readable **orange “IñigoSync” text** beside it as requested. Reuse the header's image sizing, alignment, font family, weight, and orange treatment in the mobile menu, footer, and portal system lockups; the footer tagline stays below the lockup. Use a darker orange or subtle outline on light surfaces as needed for contrast. Use `object-fit: contain` and enough height/width so none of the badge or baked-in wordmark is cut off. Check the lockup at desktop and phone sizes, including its fixed position at the top and after scrolling.
3. **Featured.** Keep “Featured events” at the top of the section, with a refreshed heading treatment and type scale. Replace the two-column image/post composition with one large event image. Put Previous and Next controls near the left and right image edges, slightly **above** the image's vertical midpoint, visible only when multiple published events exist; they must never overlap the title. Attach the event title, tag, and metadata in a compact caption at the image’s lower edge: only a **shallow 24–48px overlap on desktop** so the lower photo remains visible, stacked entirely beneath the image on narrow screens. Give the caption its own unobstructed space, including for long titles. Under the caption, center the icon-only playback control **above** a centered row of slide indicator dots; neither may collide with caption text or arrows. Show a two-bar pause **symbol**, not the word “Pause”; give the icon-only control an accessible name and show a play symbol when paused. Use a restrained raised surface, dark outline/shadow, and a local gradient behind any overlapping text for the requested 3D depth and readability. Avoid heavy effects on the photograph. Keep the event data, fallback/error states, keyboard and swipe behavior, reduced-motion support, and readable crops for portrait/landscape uploads. The separate post-like text panel is removed, not the event information.
4. **Courts & Pricing.** Keep every court as an individual clickable card with its current photo/placeholder, sport name, count, rate, and court viewer behavior. Replace the fixed four-column desktop layout with an adaptive three-column layout for nine cards, producing three complete rows. At intermediate widths use two columns and center or stretch an odd last card intentionally; use one column on small phones. If the live number of sports changes, the final row should remain balanced without blank card placeholders or data duplication.
5. **About, reviews, closing area.** Retain the About story and verified reviews. About uses a light editorial surface with dark copy in light mode, and a dark band in dark mode; the venue photo and separate business logo remain. Replace the generic “Make your next visit count” copy with a short **Plan your visit** section: clear venue/location context, an “Explore courts” link, and a **“Log in / Sign up”** button that opens the existing auth dialog. Keep this section **transparent**, with no separate background photo, dark fill, or overlay, so the page's original background shows through. The former “Call Iñigos” button is removed; phone contact remains in the footer. Preserve the map, contact details, and footer links; do not invent venue information.
6. **Typography.** Keep the existing shared Sora and Inter font files/families, but change their use: a stronger Sora display scale for section/event titles, Inter for supporting copy and controls, fewer all-caps labels, consistent line heights and letter spacing. Avoid adding a third font or making operational dashboards decorative.
7. **System themes.** Redefine the shared semantic tokens so dark mode uses black/near-black surfaces, white text, and orange actions/accents; light mode uses white/off-white surfaces, black text, and orange actions/accents. Orange should remain a clear accent in both modes, not the body-text color on white. Preserve distinct success, warning, error, and availability states. Update all public, auth/booking, customer, staff, owner, policy, onboarding, return, dialog, chart, loading, and empty/error surfaces that consume or override the shared tokens. Keep theme persistence and initial system-preference behavior. Use theme-specific opaque nav surfaces and accessible button, link, focus, border, and disabled states. Review hard-coded blue/khaki legacy colors and local overrides in `Style/LandingNight.css`, `Style/LandingPage.css`, dashboard styles, and inline styles before replacing them. Carry the sample's cleaner card/spacing hierarchy into portals without adding marketing-page photo bands to dense working screens.
