A place to play.
A place to come together.

Iñigos Sports Center has hosted games, leagues, and walk-in play in Brgy. Bocohan for over a decade. With a range of sports to choose from, there's room for a friendly match, a new hobby, or a game with your regular crew.

From basketball and badminton to bowling and billiards, find your sport and make time for the people you play with. IñigoSync makes it easy to book your next visit online.
